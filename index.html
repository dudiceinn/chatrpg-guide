CHATTARA RPG

A multiplayer RPG arena played entirely from the live chat. Your hero mines, levels up, fights monsters, and climbs the boards — all from your comments and ❤️.

📖 How to Play 🏆 Leaderboards

Your first minute

✦Say hello in the TikTok LIVE chat. Find your hero by its name and yellow ring.
✦Type exp. Your hero walks to the EXP field and fights automatically.
✦Type help, then follow the next quest above your hero.

Type commands in the live chat, not on this website. The ! prefix is optional.

Explore when you are ready
✦

Four classes

Warrior, Rogue, Mage and Ranger. Start with your hero, then explore the job menu.

✦

Quests

Follow one prompt at a time. Check daily quests with dq.

✦

Ascension & Tower

Complete ascension at level 50 to unlock progression up to level 150 and tower adventures.

✦

Ranked duels

Use pvp or pvpall to see opponents and follow the matchup prompts.

Find what you need in game

shop opens the heart shop with current items and prices. wardrobe opens your wardrobe.

gold shows your wallet. sore sells ore. sb red buys red potions.

Need more commands? Type commands, or read the guide.
