CHATTARA RPG
How to Play

Welcome to the Arena

Chattara RPG is a live arena that plays along with the chat. Your hero mines, levels up, and fights — all from the comments.

👀Watch your hero roam the world in real time.
💬Steer it with simple chat commands.
🏆Climb the leaderboard. Your progress is saved.

Use Prev / Next below (or the ← → keys) to flip through. Tara! 🚀

Step 1

How to Join

⌨️Type !join in chat — and you're in the arena.
✨Or just say anything — any comment auto-joins you instantly.
❤️Tap the heart, follow, or send a gift — that drops you in too.
↩️Been here before? Your level, XP and ore are saved.

A spotlight + glow flashes on your character when you join, so you can find yourself on the map.

Step 2

Commands

!mineDig ore at the mine.
!expHunt monsters for XP. (also !train / !level)
!restRecover HP back at the inn.
!repairFix your pickaxe. (also !fix)
!attack nameChallenge a player in the EXP field — or bonk a celebrity bot anywhere for fun.
!stopChill out and go idle. (also !idle)

The ! is optional and spaces are fine — rest, ! rest and !rest all work.

⚔️ Player vs Player

Duel Other Viewers

🤺Type !attack name to challenge another player.
📍Duels only happen in the EXP field — the rest of the map is safe.
🔁Pick your matchup: 2× damage vs the class you counter.
🏳️They fight back automatically. Last one standing wins — the loser wakes at the inn, the winner gets +5 XP.
🟥Strike first and you're a red-named PKer until you're KO'd.

For fun: you can attack the celebrity bots anywhere — they swing back, but they can't KO you. 😈
