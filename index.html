CHATTARA RPG

A multiplayer RPG arena played entirely from the live chat. Your hero mines, levels up, fights monsters, and climbs the boards — all from your comments and ❤️.

📖 How to Play 🏆 Leaderboards

Jump in — it's instant

💬Type anything in chat (even 1) and your hero spawns into the arena.
❤️Tap the heart to power the community meter, climb the ❤️ board, and earn flex wings.
⚔️Then !exp to fight, !mine to dig, !ss/!sb to trade — the Guide has the rest.
