Find your hero

Play from the TikTok LIVE chat. This website is your guide.

✦Say hello in the live chat to join.
✦Look for your name and the pulsing yellow ring around your hero.
✦Tap hearts and watch your hero react. New arrivals get a moment of camera focus.

Already playing? Jump to Commands. You do not need to repeat the introduction.

Your first action — 2 / 3

Type exp

Send this one word in the live chat: exp

✦Your hero heads to the EXP field and fights monsters automatically.
✦Watch your hero earn XP and work toward the next level.

No need to type a long sentence. exp and !exp both work.

Find help — 3 / 3

Type help

Once you have tried exp, send help in the live chat.

✦The beginner Help menu shows a small set of useful commands.
✦Then follow the next quest prompt above your hero, one action at a time.
✦Lost your hero? Type me to locate it.
✦Prefer another language? Type lang with es, id, vi or th to get help and quests in it.

Keep this handy

Everyday commands

Type one command at a time in the live chat.

✦exp — fight monsters for XP.
✦mine — head to the mine.
✦rest — recover at the inn.
✦repair — repair your pickaxe.
✦help — beginner help; commands — more options.

Commands work with or without !. Use stop to stop your current activity.

Your next step

Follow your quest

The prompt above your hero tells you what to try next.

✦quest — show or hide your current hero quest.
✦dq — check daily quests.
✦inventory — check your items.
✦gold — check your wallet.

Returning players can carry on from their saved progress. Follow the current prompt instead of starting over.

Your hero

Four classes

Warrior, Rogue, Mage and Ranger each have their own fighting style.

✦Warrior and Rogue fight up close.
✦Ranger and Mage fight from range.
✦job — open the class menu and check the available choices.

You can start playing with your current class. Equipment and skills matter as you progress.

Trade & recovery

Gold and potions

Mine ore, sell it, and prepare for your next fight.

✦sore — sell ore for gold.
✦sb red — buy red potions; check the in-game menu for current prices.
✦pots — drink a carried potion when healing is needed.
✦rest — return to the inn to recover.

Use the prices and purchase instructions shown in the game before spending.

Hearts & style

Make your hero yours

Heart taps contribute to your total and the community heart meter.

✦shop — see the current heart shop and prices.
✦hb number — buy the item with that shop number. Replace number with the number shown.
✦wardrobe — open your wardrobe.

Spending hearts does not reduce your lifetime heart leaderboard total. Follow the community meter to see the next reward.

Player vs player

Try ranked duels

Ready for a different challenge? Open the PvP menu.

✦pvp — same-class ranked opponents.
✦pvpall — ranked opponents across classes.
✦f1, f2 or f3 — choose the corresponding opponent shown.

Check the matchup and follow the fight prompts. Ranked results affect your rating.

Beyond level 50

Ascend

Reach level 50, then complete the ascension requirements to unlock progression up to level 150.

✦ascend — see your requirements and progress.
✦Follow the ascension quest, including its Treant Fruit requirement.
✦After ascending, keep building your hero through higher-level challenges.

Opening the menu does not instantly ascend your hero. Complete the requirements shown in the game.

Advanced adventures

Endless Tower

Ascended heroes can take on the tower.

✦et — begin or continue your tower run.
✦et3 — farm floor 3 once it is available to you.
✦Check the entry prompt for pass requirements and available entries.
✦During farming, tap hearts and follow the dice and wave prompts.

Start with the floor you can survive. The game shows current rewards, entry costs and wave effects.
